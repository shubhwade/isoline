Four years of statements, in seconds.

Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow come from Yahoo Finance, with SEC EDGAR as the fallback for US tickers.

Nine signals. Nine passes.

The Piotroski F-score tests profitability, leverage and liquidity, and operating efficiency, one binary signal at a time.

Distress risk, on Altman's scale.

The Z″ model for non-manufacturers: 6.56·X1 + 3.26·X2 + 6.72·X3 + 1.05·X4. Above 2.6 is the safe zone.

When data is missing, it says so.

No zeros standing in for gaps. A metric that can't be computed is withheld, with the reason printed next to it.

Project the cash.

Free cash flow starts from the three-year average ($111.7M) and grows at the 12.5% revenue CAGR, fading to 2.5% by year five.

Discount it back.

Each year is discounted at 10% (a stated default, never hidden) and the terminal value is added. Here the terminal value is 71.3% of the total.

●  Reports

A report you can hand to someone.

Every scan writes a print-ready report laid out like a company's published statements, readable by a first-time investor, plus an Excel workbook.

  • 01Statements like an annual report. Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, four years.
  • 02Every number explained. A plain-English line beside each measure, plus a glossary.
  • 03Strengths and concerns. Built only from the figures that could be computed.
  • 04Prints to A4. Self-contained and offline. Excel, JSON and CSV too.

●  Commands

Small surface. Deep output.

Yahoo Finance symbols: AAPL for US listings, INFY.NS for NSE, .BO for BSE. Every command takes --help.

~ $ isoline scan AAPL

Scan a company

Runs every engine, prints tables and a rule-based summary, writes the Excel and HTML reports and opens the report.

●  For AI tools

Give your assistant real numbers.

Run Isoline as an MCP server and Claude Desktop, Cursor or Windsurf call the same engines, so answers rest on computed figures, not recall.
