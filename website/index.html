The engines

Published models, implemented faithfully.

Each score follows its source paper, cites its inputs and states its limits. The methodology page lists every formula and data field, so you can check the work.

01

Financial health

Piotroski F-score from nine binary signals, plus the Altman Z″ distress model with safe, grey and distress zones.

Z″ = 6.56·X1 + 3.26·X2 + 6.72·X3 + 1.05·X4
02

Earnings quality

Beneish M-score across eight variables, accruals ratio and cash conversion to show when reported profit isn't turning into cash.

M = −4.84 + 0.920·DSRI + 0.528·GMI + 0.404·AQI + 0.892·SGI + …
03

Returns and growth

DuPont ROE split into margin, turnover and leverage. ROIC, FCF margin and yield, three-year revenue and profit CAGR, margin trend.

ROE = NM × AT × EMROICFCF yieldCAGR 3y
04

Valuation

P/E, P/B and EV/EBITDA, plus a discounted cash flow with bear, base and bull cases and a discount-rate by terminal-growth grid.

isoline value AAPL--discount 0.09--growth 0.07
Reports

A report you can hand to someone.

Every scan writes a formal HTML report and an Excel workbook next to you, then opens the report in your browser.

  • Self-contained HTML. Summary, score cards, explained tables and two charts. Works offline, prints cleanly to A4.
  • Multi-sheet Excel. A dashboard sheet plus the raw figures behind every metric.
  • JSON and CSV. --json or --export for pipelines, notebooks and Power BI.
ACME_dashboard.html
Honest data

Missing inputs show n/a with a reason. Never a zero, never a guess.

Free financial data has gaps. When a figure can't be computed, Isoline withholds it and says why, instead of quietly scoring a company on numbers that aren't there. US tickers fall back to SEC EDGAR when Yahoo Finance is incomplete.

Commands

Small surface, deep output.

Use Yahoo Finance symbols: AAPL for US listings, INFY.NS for NSE, .BO for BSE. Every command takes --help.

Scan a company

Runs every engine, prints the tables and summary, writes the Excel and HTML reports and opens the report.

isoline scan AAPL
isoline scan INFY.NS --no-open
isoline scan MSFT --json --no-report
Writes AAPL_analysis.xlsxAAPL_dashboard.html

Compare two companies

Every metric side by side, with a combined report.

isoline compare TCS.NS INFY.NS
isoline compare AAPL MSFT --export compare.xlsx

Estimate intrinsic value

A discounted cash flow with bear, base and bull scenarios, a sensitivity grid and margin of safety. Defaults are printed as assumptions; override any of them. Illustrative, not a price target.

isoline value AAPL
isoline value AAPL --discount 0.09 --growth 0.07 --years 10

Screen a sector

Filter an example sector universe by Piotroski score and leverage. Skipped tickers come with a reason.

isoline screen IT
isoline screen Banking --min-piotroski 7 --max-de 1.5

Work in the terminal interface

A full-screen app with modes, a command palette on Ctrl+K and session history.

pip install "isoline[tui]"
isoline tui

Export to Power BI

Portfolio metrics or red-flag details as a tidy CSV, ready to load.

isoline powerbi --tickers INFY.NS TCS.NS WIPRO.NS --export portfolio.csv
isoline powerbi AAPL MSFT --flags --export flags.csv
