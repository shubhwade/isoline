    Methodology

    Isoline reads annual statements from Yahoo Finance and reports screening statistics, not investment advice. Rules that apply everywhere:

    • A missing input gives n/a, never 0. A genuine zero stays zero.
    • A score is shown only when every input it needs exists for the required years.
    • Statement rows are looked up by canonical name, so both old and current yfinance labels work.
    • Year t is the latest fiscal year, t-1 the one before.

    Piotroski F-score (0-9)

    Piotroski (2000), Value Investing: The Use of Historical Financial Statement Information to Separate Winners from Losers, Journal of Accounting Research 38. One point per signal:

    Signal Passes when
    ROA positive Net income(t) / total assets(t-1) > 0
    CFO positive Operating cash flow(t) > 0
    ROA improving ROA(t) > ROA(t-1), each over beginning-of-year assets
    Low accruals Operating cash flow(t) > net income(t)
    Leverage falling Long-term debt / average total assets lower than last year
    Liquidity rising Current ratio(t) > current ratio(t-1)
    No dilution Shares outstanding(t) <= shares outstanding(t-1)
    Gross margin rising Gross margin(t) > gross margin(t-1)
    Asset turnover rising Revenue(t) / total assets(t-1) > same for t-1

    Needs three balance-sheet years. If any signal cannot be computed the score is withheld and the missing signals are listed. Designed for value stocks; interpret with care elsewhere.

    Altman Z″-score

    Altman (1983/1995) model for non-manufacturers and emerging markets:

    Z″ = 6.56·X1 + 3.26·X2 + 6.72·X3 + 1.05·X4

    X1 = working capital / total assets, X2 = retained earnings / total assets, X3 = EBIT / total assets, X4 = book equity / total liabilities. Zones: above 2.6 safe, 1.1-2.6 grey, below 1.1 distress. Not computed for financial firms.

    Beneish M-score

    Beneish (1999), The Detection of Earnings Manipulation, Financial Analysts Journal 55(5).

    M = -4.84 + 0.920·DSRI + 0.528·GMI + 0.404·AQI + 0.892·SGI + 0.115·DEPI - 0.172·SGAI + 4.679·TATA - 0.327·LVGI

    Index Definition
    DSRI (Receivables/Sales)(t) ÷ (Receivables/Sales)(t-1)
    GMI Gross margin(t-1) ÷ gross margin(t)
    AQI 1 - (current assets + net PP&E)/total assets ÷ same(t-1)
    SGI Sales(t) ÷ Sales(t-1)
    DEPI Dep/(Dep + net PP&E) ÷ same(t)
    SGAI (SG&A/Sales)(t) ÷ (SG&A/Sales)(t-1)
    LVGI (current liabilities + long-term debt)/total assets ÷ same(t-1)
    TATA (Net income - operating cash flow) / total assets(t)

    TATA uses the cash-flow form of total accruals. A score above -2.22 raises a high-severity flag (Beneish's paper also discusses -1.78, which flags fewer companies). Not computed for financial firms.

    Ratios and flags

    • Debt / equity is total liabilities / shareholders' equity. Not reported when equity <= 0; negative equity raises its own high-severity flag.
    • Interest coverage = EBIT / |interest expense|. EBITDA is never substituted for EBIT.
    • Negative FCF flag: free cash flow (operating cash flow minus |capex|) negative in 2+ of the last 3 years.
    • Liabilities/equity > 2 raises a medium flag.

    Known limitations

    Annual data only; no restatement tracking; no currency conversion (ratios are unit-free, absolute values are in the reporting currency); fiscal years are not calendar-aligned across companies; sector peer valuation is not implemented; data quality is bounded by Yahoo Finance.

    Quality metrics (v0.3.3, isoline/core/engines/quality.py)

    All flow items are for the latest fiscal year; return denominators use the average of year-end and prior year-end balances. Missing inputs give n/a plus a note, never zero, and no tax rate is assumed.

    Metric Formula
    Net margin Net income / Revenue
    Asset turnover Revenue / Average total assets
    Equity multiplier Average total assets / Average equity
    ROE (DuPont) Net margin x Asset turnover x Equity multiplier = Net income / Average equity (n/a if average equity <= 0)
    ROIC EBIT x (1 - effective tax rate) / Average invested capital, where invested capital = equity + total debt - cash and effective tax rate = tax provision / pretax income (n/a for financials or if the tax rate is unavailable)
    FCF Operating cash flow - abs(capex); FCF margin = FCF / Revenue; FCF / Net income only when net income > 0
    Accruals ratio (Net income - Operating cash flow) / Average total assets (Sloan, 1996)
    DSO / DIO / DPO Receivables x 365 / Revenue; Inventory x 365 / COGS; Payables x 365 / COGS (year-end balances)
    Cash conversion cycle DSO + DIO - DPO; n/a unless all three are computable (n/a for financials)
    Share count change (Shares t - Shares t-1) / Shares t-1

    Verification: every row is covered by a hand-computed test in tests/test_scores.py, including the DuPont identity. Not yet verified against live provider data.
